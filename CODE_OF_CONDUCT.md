# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive and harassment-free project for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive and specific.
- Respect different backgrounds and levels of experience.
- Discuss ideas and evidence rather than attacking people.
- Protect private or licensed source material.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate publication of private data, and repeated bad-faith disruption are not accepted.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors and users.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant principles.
